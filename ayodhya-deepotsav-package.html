Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Rs 5,999 Ayodhya Deepotsav package?

Pickup and drop, 2 nights stay on double or triple sharing at Aadvik Residency, a private vehicle with driver, toll and parking, Sugam Darshan Pass for Ram Mandir on Day 1, and local sightseeing as per the itinerary. Meals are not included.

Is the price per person? Is there a minimum group size?

Yes, all rates are per person on double or triple sharing. The package rates of Rs 5,999 and Rs 8,999 are for groups of 4 or more guests. For smaller groups, message us and we will share the available options.

Are meals included?

No. Meals are not part of the package price. Pure vegetarian satvik meals can be arranged on request at Aadvik Residency.

What dates does the package cover?

The standard dates are 6 to 8 November 2026 for the Ayodhya package and 6 to 9 November 2026 for the Ayodhya plus Varanasi package. Different dates can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

How does Sugam Darshan work, and is it guaranteed?

We arrange a Sugam Darshan Pass for Ram Mandir on Day 1 for fast-track entry. Passes and darshan timings are controlled by the temple trust and the administration, so they are subject to availability and may change on the day. We will update you if anything changes.

Will I get a seat in the VIP gallery for the diya lighting?

We cannot promise official VIP gallery passes, because they are issued by the administration in limited numbers. What we do is guide you to the best accessible vantage point for the Saryu Maha Aarti, diya lighting and the drone show, and confirm the meeting point closer to the date.

How many diyas and drones are planned for Deepotsav 2026?

The administration has announced plans for 30 lakh or more diyas and a 2,100-drone show, along with a Saryu Maha Aarti with 1,100 priests and 3D projection and laser shows. These are announced plans and the final numbers and timings may change.

How do I book, and how much do I pay to confirm?

Send your details on WhatsApp or call 9956860590. A 30% token amount confirms your booking. We then send your confirmation with pickup details.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

If you cancel at least 5 days before the travel date, you receive a full refund of the amount paid. After that, no refund is given.

What is the early-bird offer?

Bookings confirmed by 1 November 2026 can save up to 20%. Message us with your group size and dates and we will tell you the exact saving.

Where is Aadvik Residency located?

Aadvik Residency is 2 km from Ayodhya Dham Junction and 3 km from Ayodhya Airport, on the Varanasi-Faizabad National Highway (Raibarelly Road). Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi and Saryu Ghat are within 10 to 15 minutes in normal traffic.

Can you pick us up from Varanasi?

Yes. For the Ayodhya plus Varanasi package we can arrange pickup from or drop at Varanasi Airport or Varanasi Cantt Station. Choose it in the form or tell us on WhatsApp.

What should we carry for the Deepotsav evening?

Comfortable footwear, a light jacket for the cool evening and a power bank. Roads near Ram Ki Paidi close before dusk, so the evening is on foot. Phones and bags may not be allowed inside the Ram Mandir complex, so lockers are used.
